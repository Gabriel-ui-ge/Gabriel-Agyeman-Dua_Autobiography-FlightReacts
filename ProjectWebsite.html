About Me

FlightReacts, whose legal name is Kimani Tariq Kamiru-White, is a highly popular American YouTuber and streamer. Born on August 7, 1995, and originally from Washington, D.C., he later grew up in West Palm Beach, Florida. He is best known for his energetic and highly expressive reaction videos, particularly covering NBA basketball highlights, viral internet trends, and video games.

Early Life

Born Kamani Tariq Kamiru-White on August 7, 1995, in Washington, D.C., FlightReacts moved to West Palm Beach, Florida at age one. Raised by a photographer father and stay-at-home mother, he initially focused on basketball and music before turning to YouTube in 2013 to overcome depression and find digital stardom. A more detailed look at the roots and early challenges that shaped his energetic, viral personality on YouTube: Childhood: Grew up mostly in Florida, facing a tough middle school environment surrounded by bullying and gang violence.Early Setbacks: Suffered a high ankle sprain in high school that ended his basketball dreams, leading to a difficult period involving dropping out of Palm Beach State College and working fast-food jobs. The "Flight" Nickname: Coined in the tenth grade, the moniker originated because he spent much of his time smoking marijuana to cope with the depression of his injuries and personal struggles.YouTube Beginnings: Started with gaming and reaction videos, eventually exploding in popularity with his unfiltered, enthusiastic reactions to NBA highlights specifically those of his favorite player, Stephen Curry.

YouTube Journey

Before the fame, Kimani worked at Steak 'n Shake and Chick-fil-A. In 2013, he turned to YouTube as a creative outlet. Starting as "Mr big Boy 305," he eventually evolved into "NotYourAverageFlight," becoming a pillar of the 2K and reaction communities. The Name Origin: He got the nickname "Flight" in high school because he frequently came to class high. When he started playing NBA 2K and Call of Duty on the Xbox 360, it naturally became his gamertag.Gaming & Basketball Roots: He built his initial following by playing NBA 2K and uploading 1v1 basketball videos against other creators like Jesser and RiceGum.Reaction Empire: During the 2020 pandemic, an NBA partnership allowed him to react to live game highlights. His passionate, often exaggerated reactions to players like Stephen Curry became his signature content.Music Career: Flight expanded into music, releasing rap tracks and EPs. His hit song "Disingenuous" gained millions of views across digital platforms."June Flight": An iconic catchphrase and running joke where he confidently claims he will get into peak physical shape and dominate in basketball by the summer.

Music Career

Flight’s music journey began as a secondary outlet for his basketball and internet persona, characterized by quotable lyrics and a heavy dose of his signature high-energy personality."Disingenuous": Widely considered his most popular track, amassing millions of views across platforms.Recent Releases: He has dropped tracks like Let Her Win (featuring Skilla Baby), Bet Dat, Winner Soldier, and Pushing 30 Flow.Streaming and Music PresenceFans and internet culture creators frequently react to his music drops, making his tracks a staple in the reaction community.
